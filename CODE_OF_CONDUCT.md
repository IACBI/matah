# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Matah to be a welcoming project for players and contributors of every
background and experience level. Participation should be respectful, safe, and
focused on constructive collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent while addressing the actual impact of words and actions.
- Respect different identities, cultures, languages, abilities, and viewpoints.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and accept maintainer decisions
  gracefully.
- Protect private information, including security reports and player data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing, and publishing another person's
private information are not tolerated. The same applies to bad-faith security
testing against the public demo or other users.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, and whether harmful
behavior is repeated.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile. Do not use a public issue when a report contains private information.
Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will
be handled as confidentially as practical, and retaliation against a reporter
is prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly
representing the project, including community events and project-related direct
communication.
